EVENT RECAP July 1st, 2026 • Festus Iyayi Hall

Management Colloquium & Innovation Challenge 2026 Records Massive Success

The Management Sciences Students Association (MASSA), under the visionary leadership of The Momentous Executives, successfully hosted the highly anticipated Management Colloquium & Innovation Challenge 2026 at Festus Iyayi Hall.

Centering on the theme "From Campus to Industry: Innovation, Employability & Leadership," the flagship conference brought together hundreds of high-aspiring students, academic leads, and notable industry figures.

Keynote insights were delivered by esteemed leaders including Mr. Olorunyomi Joseph Ogunleye (Head, Edo Job Centre), Barr. Jobina Arinze (Head, Edo Innovates), Dr. Mrs. Osasere Gloria Iyamu (Assistant Dean), Dr. Victor Amadin Idehen (HOD, Entrepreneurship), and Janet Iyangbe Genesis (Founder, Hyper Impact Community).

The event equipped management students with actionable career strategies, digital tools, and pitch dynamics needed to transition smoothly from university academics into competitive global markets.
