FACULTY HERITAGE

History of the Faculty

The official story of vision, rapid academic evolution, and a persistent, multi-decade struggle for institutional identity at the University of Benin.

1971 – 1982

The Genesis

Management education at UNIBEN dates back to 1971 with the creation of the Department of Economics and Business Studies. This pioneer unit offered combined honors degrees blending economic theory with foundational business concepts.

As student enrollment grew, specialized departments began to emerge:

  • October 1977: Department of Business Administration officially emerged, breaking away from Economics.
  • 1981/1982 Session: Department of Accounting was carved out to address growing demand for professional accountants.
1983 – 2005

The Long Struggle for Autonomy

The push for an independent identity began in 1983 under VC Prof. Adamu Baikie. Staff and students advocated for a separate Faculty to break free from the constraints of Social Sciences.

The tipping point arrived when National Universities Commission (NUC) accreditation panels noted accounting and business programs were overloaded with social science courses, advising UNIBEN to establish a dedicated Faculty of Management Sciences.

AUGUST 30, 2006

The Birth of the Faculty

The UNIBEN Senate officially approved splitting the Faculty of Social Sciences during its 195th regular meeting. On October 1, 2006, the Faculty came into official existence with three pioneer pillars:

  1. Department of Accounting
  2. Department of Business Administration
  3. Department of Banking and Finance

Mr. N. O. Omozusi served as pioneer Faculty Officer (2006–2008).

PRESENT DAY

Modern Evolution & Expansion

To meet modern global corporate needs, foundational departments unbundled into specialized branches including Marketing, Actuarial Sciences, Insurance, Taxation, Human Resource Management, and Entrepreneurship.
